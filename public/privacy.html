Terms of Use

Who we are

This policy describes how WEEHS handles personal information in the WEEHS Occupational Health & Safety Management System (the app) and in the WhatsApp and Telegram site-lookup bots that answer questions about a centre. WEEHS is the operator. Contact info@weehs.org or +91 77939 66625.

What this covers

It covers the app at the address where you sign in, and the bots that reply with a centre’s safety summary. It does not change the duties of the organisation that employs you or that owns the workplace records. That organisation decides what is entered about its sites and its people.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Name, email address, organisation, role, and the credentials and security settings used to sign in.
  • Phone numbers authorised for a bot. If you link WhatsApp, we store the number you authorise so the bot can recognise it and answer only for centres you can already open in the app.
  • Messages sent to a bot. WhatsApp and Telegram messages you send (a centre id or name, a menu command, or a location you choose to share) are received so the bot can reply. The lookup record we keep is the result of that lookup — which centre, and whether it was answered — not a transcript of the message.
  • Site and safety records. Information the organisation keeps in the app: sites, people associated with them, and occupational safety records such as incidents, inspections, equipment, actions, training, and similar workplace records.
  • Location, if you share it. If you send your location to a bot, it is used to suggest nearby centres for that request. It is not stored as a location history.

Why we use it

We use this information to run the app and the bots: to authenticate you, to show a centre only to someone allowed to see it, to keep the occupational safety records the organisation maintains, to send operational email about those records, and to answer a bot lookup. We do not use it for advertising.

Where it is processed and stored

The app is hosted on Google Firebase and Google Cloud. Authentication, the database, file storage, and the server functions that send mail and answer the bots run there. Those services process and store the information in order to operate WEEHS OHSMS. Google acts as the infrastructure provider for that processing.

We do not sell personal information

WEEHS does not sell personal information. We do not share it with a broker or an advertiser. We share it with the organisation whose workspace it belongs to, with people that organisation has authorised, and with the service providers who host and deliver the product, only so the product can run.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while the account exists and as needed to close it. Site and safety records are kept while the organisation uses the service, and longer where occupational safety law or that organisation’s own rules require the record to be kept. Bot lookup records are kept for a limited period and then deleted. A phone number stays linked only until you unlink it or the account is closed.

How we protect it

The app is served over HTTPS. Access to a centre depends on the role and the sites the organisation has granted. Bot replies use that same limit. Secrets used to run the service are not written into the application repository.

Your rights and deletion requests

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Some workplace safety records cannot be erased where the law requires them to be kept; where that applies we will say so and limit what we still use. Send a request to info@weehs.org. If your organisation administers the workspace, its administrator may need to act on records it owns.

Changes

If this policy changes, the page at this address will show the new text and a new effective date.
